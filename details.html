The pitch

Once a working print house in Porto's Bonfim district, this loft keeps its original steel columns and tall factory windows while everything else has been rebuilt around quiet, considered living. The main floor opens onto a rooftop terrace with a clear view over the tiled roofs toward the river.

Mornings are best spent with coffee on the terrace; the neighbourhood wakes up slowly, and the nearest bakery opens at seven. In the evening, it's a ten-minute walk down to the Ribeira, or a shorter one to any of the small tascas along Rua do Bonfim.

The space sleeps four across two bedrooms — one with a queen bed, the other with two singles that can be joined on request. Note that the building has no elevator; the loft is on the third floor.
